Cost of Inaction

What Happens When a Website Is Not Maintained

Skipping maintenance can look like a saving. The cost usually shows up later, as a hacked site, lost enquiries, or an emergency repair.

Outdated software

Plugins, themes, and your CMS release security fixes regularly. When updates are missed for months, known weaknesses stay open, and attackers look for exactly those flaws.

Risk & Consequence

Known security exploits remain unpatched, increasing vulnerability to malicious automated injection scripts.

Slow pages

Uncompressed images, a bloated database, and missing caching slow a site down over time. Visitors tend to leave slow pages, especially on mobile, and search engines take note.

Risk & Consequence

Higher bounce rates on mobile and lost organic ranking as search engines favor fast Core Web Vitals.

Broken forms and links

Forms can fail without anyone noticing, links can lead nowhere, and third-party connections can expire. A visitor who tries to enquire and hits an error rarely tries a second time.

Risk & Consequence

Direct loss of high-intent customer leads and degraded user trust when booking or quote forms fail silently.

No reliable backups

Backups that are old, or stored on the same server as the website, may not survive an attack or a hosting failure. Without a recent off-site backup, recovery can mean starting again from scratch.

Risk & Consequence

Severe downtime and catastrophic data loss if hosting servers fail or ransomware locks web databases.

Compliance & Data Protection Responsibility:

If your website collects personal data through forms or checkout, you are responsible for protecting it. India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 sets obligations for businesses handling citizen data, including reasonable security safeguards to prevent personal data breaches.
