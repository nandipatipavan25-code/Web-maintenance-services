The Cost of Neglect

What Happens When a Website Is Not Maintained

Most businesses only think about their website when it goes down. In practice, websites deteriorate gradually long before they fail completely.

Problem 1: Outdated Plugins

Vulnerable Code & Hack Exposure

Business Impact:
Security Vulnerabilities & Google Blacklisting

Over 95% of compromised websites are breached through outdated plugins and themes. Once hacked, Google displays prominent "Deceptive Site Ahead" security warnings that immediately destroy 90%+ of your web traffic.

Problem 2: Slow Loading Speed

Creeping Bloat & Performance Loss

Business Impact:
Lost Visitors, High Bounce Rates & Dropped Enquiries

53% of mobile users abandon a website that takes longer than 3 seconds to load. Uncompressed uploads, cluttered databases, and inefficient scripts silently drain your conversion rates and inflate your Google Ads cost.

Problem 3: Broken Forms & Links

Silent Technical Malfunctions

Business Impact:
Missed Business Opportunities & Wasted Ad Budget

Following automatic server or PHP updates, lead capture forms, payment checkouts, and booking calendars frequently break silently. Visitors attempt to contact you, encounter errors, and bounce to competitors without leaving a trace.

Problem 4: No Regular Backups

Zero Recovery Safety Net

Business Impact:
Catastrophic Risk of Total Data Loss & Days of Downtime

Web hosting providers rarely take comprehensive, off-site daily backups. When a server failure or accidental deletion occurs, restoring a website without a recent clean snapshot can cost upwards of ₹25,000+ with zero guarantee of recovery.
